Quest Catalog

This catalog contains all quests available at the DepthRangers guild.

The quests categories are described in the quest table below:

Quest Table

Roll 1d6 to obtain a quest:

  5. Rescue The Hostages From The Dragon

Quest 1 - Hunt Down The Orc Leader

An particularly fearless and fierce orc has been rallying troops with the intention to take over the settlement. You're tasked with hunting him down before he raises a sufficiently sized army to attack the surface. The clerk finally gives you a warning:

Unfortunately, there are no further reports on this suspect. No known weaknesses that could be exploited, so be on your toes for this one!

You crack your knuckles and head to the entrance.

In this quest, quest encounters take place starting at level 0. Once you have reached the appropriate level, the quest encounter triggers a boss fight.

Quest Encounter - You find a garrison of orcs, you fight (1+(party_size)/2) orcs with (dungeon_level+1) HP

Orc Leader: BOSS, (party_size+dungeon_level)d6 HP, and produces (dungeon_level+1)d6 damage. This ruthless opponent has (dungeon_level+party_size/3) orc minions by his side. These minions are normal Orcs that have 1 HP and do (dungeon_level+1)+1d6 damage.

Armored: The Orc leader has (dungeon_level+1) AC.

Upon reaching the surface: You place the leader's tusks on the counter and people fall silent. You are paid (350*character_level) g each, and receive recognition as a champion. You have leveled up.

Rescue An NPC

From kidnapped villagers to unwise adventurers, you may find yourself involved in a rescue operation. While the specifics lie with the person in question, in general the idea is to go in, find the person, then reach the surface with said person in good health. Failing to protect the person means failing the quest.

Roll a d6 to find out who needs your assistance to find its way out:

NPC Table

  1. Archaeologist
  2. Child
  3. Old Adventurer
  4. Alchemist
  5. Damsel
  6. Hero

Once you reach the appropriate level, the quest event will involve finding the NPC you must rescue.

NPC Rescue 1 - Archaeologist

You are tasked with retrieving an archaeologist. Apparently the archaologist wandered off during a research expedition. The clerk says in a relaxed tone:

As you know, there are so many wondrous things in the depths and academics are often not very good at being reasonable. Just bring that bookworm back in one piece.

Quest Event: You find the archaeologist. The Archaeologist however, is frail of health and can't keep up with the party. You must slow down your pace, the party will expend 2 non combat events each time you travel to a visited location (including the stairs) for the purposes of hazard die rolls (if you have a diseased or poisoned member, this handicap does not increase).

Upon reaching the surface: The archaeologist is very grateful and says that you will be commended for your efforts though unfortunately there's no financial reward. 3 months later you receive a letter of recommendation from the academy for a low skill position as a grounds keeper. You shrug and toss it aside. You managed to level up.

NPC Rescue 2 - Child

You enter the guild, a couple are sobbing next to the clerk. The clerk turns and asks that you retrieve their child. In a somber tone she adds:

A lost dog, and a lack of wisdom are all you need for a child to loose its bearings inside the bowels of the dungeon. Surprisingly, the child has a talisman that has kept the horrors at bay but it won't hold for much longer. So make the rescue before we have a casualty.

Quest Event: You found the child. This child is afraid of vermin, if you're attacked by spiders, or bats it will run away and need to be found in that level again in a visited room. If the child runs away see searching for the child.

Searching for the child: Enter a room and roll a d6, you find the child on a 5 or 6. If you find the child, ignore the hazard die roll for that one room. Don't immediately search in a room you already searched.

Upon reaching the surface: Two parents in tears greet you at the entrance, you're paid 5 g and level up. Not all rewards are financial in this world. You managed to level up.

NPC Rescue 3 - Old Adventurer

The clerk looks at you begins talking as she sorts some papers:

"Some people can't come to terms with the passing of time, and so this old timer took its gear and went in. Nobody would bat an eye if the will were not stored in that armor, hence the pious second child who would not inherit has ordered you to return that old timer alive if possible and that will at all costs."

Quest Event: You find the old timer who turns out to be much stronger than any of you, has (party_size+2*character_level)d6 HP more than your party and is able to attack using 2*(character_level)d6 while casting Restore Vitality once per fight, and is capable of double attacks. However, his blasted helmet has a foul charm that forces you to throw a Hazard die every tile for every revisited room (essentially moving equates to 4 non combat events). And, only has (dungeon_level+1)d6 rations. Not to mention, he counts as a party member for all intents and purposes meaning all your encounters will be tougher.
Upon reaching the surface: The old timer invites you to his home for a pint and rest near the fireplace where the old timer throws the will. The next morning, you're offered (200*character_level) g each. You managed to level up.

NPC Rescue 4 - Alchemist

You are taken to a separate room where the guild master describes a delicate situation. It seems that a well known alchemist has decided wander on its own into the depths of the dungeon and there are concerns, financial and influential concerns, about the well being of said alchemist. You're tasked with easing those concerns by retrieving the lost researcher.

Upon reaching the Alchemist: Crying in a corner like a lost child, an alchemist says in a wobbly voice: "I failed because I succeeded, I'm a fool because I'm a genius, what a sad day this triumph is my worst defeat ever". After a few more contrasting praises and derision, he explains that he succeeded in creating a Golem that can be disassembled but not destroyed and it won't let him out.

The Golem will be chasing you on your way out, all the way to the entrance (luckily, it's bound to the dungeon).

Instead of a Hazard dice, roll to see the if Golem has found you:

Roll Outcome
5-6 Thankfully it was just your nerves, best to keep moving.
3-4 The Golem is behind you, you can engage or attempt to flee.
1-2 The Golem is in front of you, no chance to escape you will have to fight.

The Golem has (dungeon_level+1)*2 HP and does the same amount of damage. When struck by the Golem roll a save against magic of (dungeon_level+1) (using the dungeon_level where Golem was initially found) or be paralyzed until combat concludes. The damage it does depends on the level it was found and does not decrease as you approach the surface.

Upon defeat, the Golem remains immobile until the next hazard die roll (it malfunctions temporarily, but resumes operating shortly).

Upon reaching the surface: The guards are ready to apprehend the alchemist, when a very ornate chariot from the treasury arrives to take the alchemist away. You are handed (1000*character_level) g and told you did not witness anything. You managed to level up.

NPC Rescue 5 - Damsel

The clerk looks at you and says:

"A prank held by her younger brother who proposed a tryst inside the dungeon. The child is reported to have said: "I never thought she'd fall for it". The family of this young lady has requested your services, her fiance says she can stay in there for all he cares."

Well time to put chivalry to the test.

Upon finding the Damsel: throw a d6 to determine what happens...

Damsel Status Table

Roll Outcome
5-6 Weeping and afraid she can't leave this dungeon quickly enough.
3-4 She's grateful for your visit, however there is a lesser vampire who's determined to stop you. You fight a lesser vampire.
1-2 Her gorgeous smile is ruined by the enormous fangs protruding from it. Soon a lesser vampire joins her, you fight two lesser vampires. You must somehow survive this!

Upon reaching the surface:
If she's still alive: the family takes her away violently and with a look of disgust throws you a sack of gems worth (300*character_level) g. You managed to level up.
If she was turned into a vampire: The family refuses your claims despite the evidence and their influence leaves the guild with no recourse but consider your mission failed. The guild master apologizes but mentions how not doing so would create a lot of problems for the guild. You're still offered a pint and a chance to rest. But you didn't level up or earn any money, this makes the ale taste rather bitter.

NPC Rescue 6 - Hero

The hero was last seen 2 weeks ago, and due to protocol you are now faced with a search and retrieve mission.

Once found: The hero was bored and looking for a challenge and is going back merely for a change of scenery. Upon meeting the hero, you can consider that quest won as the hero dispatches all enemies but takes any loot found.

Upon reaching the surface: You're rewarded (100*character_level) g, and level up. While successful, you wonder... Was this really a victory?

Quest 3 - Retrieve The Green Armor

Runes have been found pointing to the legendary green armor used by the ancient dwarfs in the battle against the shadow fiends.

First descend to the appropriate level, then the quests events will take place - you will find clues, and decipher them to discover the armor.

Quest Event1: You encounter a glyph in the wall, roll a magic save to successfully decipher the glyphs.
On Success: The arrows seem to be pointing you in a definite direction, move to Quest event 2.
On Failure: These glyphs are too vague, you must search for more clues before having a definite answer. Repeat Quest Event 1.

Quest Event2: The glyphs told you about a secret passage, and how to understand it. Travel to a room with an unobstructed secret door, then roll to discover the passage.
On Success: This passage is exactly as described in the glyphs you're on the right track!
On Failure: This can't be it, must keep searching. Continue looking for a room with an unobstructed secret passage.

Quest Event3: You have finally found it, the legendary green armor. But... something's wrong, there's a weird shadow that keeps moving on top of it. A shadow creature wearing the armor rises and attacks!

Armored Shadow creature: (party_size+dungeon_level)d6 HP immune to sneak attacks and sedation, +3 AC. While it lacks a blade, it flails its arms and is able to attack proficiently attacking as if it had a single handed weapon causing (dungeon_level)d6 HP damage.

Undead: The Armored Shadow is an undead creature.

This creature is vulnerable to Aura of Light and Illumination spells, they make it unable to hold on to the armor (it looses its 3 AC).

Once defeated, you hold the mythical green armor. It's a +(3+dungeon_level) AC unique armor, that molds to the body shape of the wearer and is extremely light (anybody can use it). You're free to wear it on the way back. So time to make your way to the surface.

Upon reaching the surface: It's tempting to hold on to the armor, however if you decided to keep it you'd have fight of the guild and perhaps the entire army so suffice to say once you hand it over to the guild who themselves hand it over to a rather scrawny noble who'll never see combat in his lifetime. Still you're rewarded (100*character_level) g each, and leveled up. Could be worse.

Quest 4 - Exchange Prisoner Elgar

You receive a letter from the captain of the guard:

Report to the Central Prison for a prisoner transfer, details upon arrival. Be sure to equip yourselves with extra rations.

Upon arriving at the prison, you are directed to the appropriate cell by a guard, the cellar is standing next to the door smiling. You receive a series of indications that describe what you need to do and where you need to go. The prisoner is bound by chains in hands and feet, you are given 1d6 bandages (roll to determine how many), then the guard tells you:

You will deliver someone and retrieve someone, both of which wish to escape, and that can be a big problem if they do. Since you won't be allowed back out or at the drop off location until you find your inmate. Also, you are tasked with keeping the prisoner alive, if the prisoner escapes, DO NOT kill it during recapture! I repeat, you must make sure the prisoner does not escape or die during transit. Cellar if you'd do the honors...

The cellar clears his throat and puts the key in the cell door. Then the guard continues:

You have the privilege of escorting Half-Elf Elgar. He's wanted for high treason here, but he's equally wanted for high treason in his homeland. However, they have a dark magus who needs to be interrogated and the cellar is only too eager to provide assistance (yeesh, some things you rather not know about). The job is to take him to this location...

Prisoner you will deliver:
Elgar: 2d6 HP, Elf Rogue.

Prisoner you will receive in exchange:
Morton: (2d6-dungeon_level) (at least 3), Human Mage. Knows Stop and Invisibility.

Pleased to meet your acquaintance!

Says Elgar sarcastically! You brush it off and make your way to the entrance.

Recapture Mechanism: Should the prisoner escape, during recapture Elgar will inflict 1d6 damage each round. To recapture Elgar, you must roll a 5 or 6 to incapacitate - he'll receive 1HP damage. If you roll a 1, then you will inflict 1d6 damage and the prisoner will escape injured. You have two roundsj during recapture before he escapes, in which case you must locate the prisoner again and begin the process anew.

Hazard die rolls: If you're revisiting rooms, the prisoner will delay your efforts to reach your destination. The party will expend 2 non combat events each time you travel to a visited location (including the stairs). Should the inmate die, you will carry the corpse which slows you even further so you'll have to throw a hazard die every room. In which case, perhaps you could revive the prisoner, at least before handing him over?

Traps and inmate health: Traps that are set off will also affect the inmate, so be careful!

Rest and lunch breaks: Since you have a prisoner who wishes to escape, you can't rest until you have delivered the exchanged prisoner. This includes eating at the staircase.

Quest Events while escorting Elgar: The prisoner attempts to escape, roll a dex save to stop the prisoner in time. If the Prisoner has escaped, ignore these events until he's captured.

Quest Encounters while Escorting Elgar: If the Prisoner is in custody ignore the encounters, otherwise you find the prisoner.

Upon reaching the level, you look for an unobstructed secret door and roll a magic save. If you succeed, you notice a keyhole. Roll a lock-picking check if you succeed again, go to on success.

On Success: You open the door to meet a series of armed guards at the ready. They all look elven, but their skin is dark. The one at the front motions you to drop your weapons to the floor.

If you have the prisoner - alive: One of the guards pulls on Elgar's chains then uses the momentum to ram the hilt of his sword straight onto his jaw splattering blood and pieces of teeth on the floor. With an effortless motion he proceeds to carry him unconscious. The rest of the guards put their weapons to your throats but remain otherwise immobile. After a moderate wait period, you see a very beaten up man. In addition to manacles, he's also gagged. Time to make your way back.
If you have the prisoner - dead: The front guard collects the body, pulling it by the hair and tossing it to the other guards. You are given a letter formally refusing the exchange. You must make your way back.
If you don't have the prisoner: The front guard collects your weapons then sends an arrow straight onto your shoulder, you suffer 1d6HP damage. The wall then closes - The message is clear, find the prisoner before returning! What a day...

On your way back: Much like Elgar, Morton does not wish to arrive to his destination, and will put up a much greater fight.

Quest Events while escorting Morton: The prisoner attempts to escape his gag, roll a dex save to stop the prisoner in time. If the Prisoner manages to escape the gag he will immediately cast Stop on you and become invisible then run away. Morton has escaped, ignore further events until he's captured again.

Quest Encounters while Escorting Morton: If the Prisoner is in custody ignore the encounters, otherwise you find the prisoner. Morton inflicts 2 damage, you must roll a 5 or 6 to incapacitate him, but he'll receive 1 HP damage. On a 1 you inflict 1d6 damage and he escapes injured (if he survives). You have one round to capture him before he turn invisible and escapes.

Upon reaching the surface:
If you deliver Morton alive: You finally reach the prison. The cellar greets you at the entrance with a small garrison. He thanks you for your efforts and the many happy nights ahead, meanwhile you can see Morton's pale complexion upon hearing about this... Seems the cellar has quite a reputation! You return to the guild and receive (300*character_level) g each, and an invitation to the party later that night by the cellar. While it's always nice for people to remember you fondly, you decide you rather party with a tankard full of ale and forget this incident. You managed to level up.

If you deliver Morton dead: You reach a security outpost and deliver the body, then head to the guild. The clerk looks at you and hands over (50*character_level) g each then tells you to lay low for a while since the cellar is particularly upset. It would be wise to wait until he receives a new playmate rather than to prance around the streets carelessly. You have failed the quest and do not advance a level.
If you deliver the Elven letter: You reach a security outpost and deliver the letter, then head to the guild. The clerk tells you to leave and make sure to have your affairs in order in case of an inspection which should happen regularly. Also to keep a close eye in case a soldier wishes to plant a bogus charge, then he charges you (15*character_level) g each for the advice. You have failed the quest and do not advance a level.

Quest 5 - Rescue The Hostages From The Dragon

You enter the guild and a distressed clerk reads:

Claims of people in the outskirts going missing were ignored until the first born of the captain of the guards who was traveling the outskirts of the city was rumored to be taken away - he's not been seen since. Your job is to determine what's happening, why it's happening, and then put a stop to it!

The clerk leaves the documentation behind as she leaves. You take your documents and head to the entrance.

You must first descend to your target level.

Upon reaching the level, you must explore the rooms and trigger quest events. This way, you can begin the process of tracking down the location.

Quest Encounter: You fight a Baby Dragon.

Baby Dragon: (dungeon_level+2)d6 HP, Fire breath deals (dungeon_level+2)d6 on a single party member.

Armored: Baby dragons have tough skin that grants them (dungeon_level+1) AC.

Quest Event1 : Burn marks on the ground tell you that you're in the right vicinity. Though you feel for the victims, this time you're not as eager to find your target.

Quest Event2 : You find treasure , but it's molten on the floor. Quite a travesty!

Quest Event3 : You hear heavy breathing, luckily you notice a small hatch, and decide to crawl in. The breathing sound grows louder, this must not be a "typical" dragon, not that such niceties would make you at ease. You eventually reach a hatch, and lift it slowly, just enough to peer through the crack. The victims seem to be tied there. One of them is missing, well its arms are still there but the rest is gone. The others seem to be in one piece. You take your chances and gently open it to escape, you make a silence sign to the prisoners though they're mostly unconscious. To free them, you must unlock these chains, roll an unlock check 3 times. For each failed lock check, roll a body save; roll a hazard die upon failure.

After freeing the prisoners: You manage to get into the hatch with them when you hear a deafening roar, it takes some time for the ringing to calm down enough to hear the thrashing that follows. At least you're in a good hiding place, but the beast knows where that is.

Roll (dungeon_level+1) consecutive dex saves to sprint as quickly as you can out of here, each time you fail, fire damage will singe you causing (dungeon_level+1)d6 HP damage.

After escaping the crawl space: You make a sigh of relief then hear a massive charge against the wall. It held, but for how long? You must keep running out of here! Make your way out of this level. Roll a hazard dice every square, each time you land on 2, roll a body save or suffer (dungeon_level +1) damage from the heat of the dragon increasing the temperature of the entire level. Once you reach the stairs, escape returns to normal.

Upon reaching the surface: You take the victims straight to the pub and all of you have a cold beverage, it still feels like you are back in that furnace of a level. Then you go to the guild, and a security officer goes along with a clerk to help and collect their testimony. Meanwhile you receive a (150*character_level) g reward each, the Guild master is about to protest about taking the victims to the pub then looks at the sooth in your clothes, hair and eyes and decides to hand you and additional 100 g instead. This time, you simply go to bed and call it a day. Congratulations, you managed to level up.

Quest 6 - Portal Peril

You found a peculiar device at ground level. Unfortunately, you tend to touch things that interest you, and soon you were a level deeper than your party level. To make things worse, you hear a roaring at a distance.

You must escape and quickly !

(the adventurers don't know and can't really know, that there is a massive flood as that device has potentially connected a lagoon with the lower end of the dungeon).

You have (12*dungeon_level) turns to reach level 0 or drown. Once there, you must activate the quest event to complete this quest.

Quest event: You find the room with the portal and undo the changes that activated it. You make your way out, keep reading below.

Upon reaching the surface: You describe the events and soon all wizards start getting agitated, some panicking other excited, and others lost in thought. Meanwhile, you collect your acceptable (200*character_level) g each then head to the pub. You managed to level up.
