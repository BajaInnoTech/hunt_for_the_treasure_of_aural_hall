### Quest 1 - Hunt Down The Orc Leader

An particularly fearless and fierce orc has been rallying troops with the intention to take over the settlement. You're tasked with hunting him down before he raises a sufficiently sized army to attack the surface. The clerk finally gives you a warning:

> Unfortunately, there are no further reports on this suspect. No known weaknesses that could be exploited, so be on your toes for this one!

You crack your knuckles and head to the entrance.

_In this quest, quest encounters take place starting at level 0. Once you have reached the appropriate level, the quest encounter triggers a boss fight._

_Quest Encounter_ - You find a garrison of orcs, you fight _(1+(party\_size)/2)_ orcs with _(dungeon\_level+1)_ HP

**Orc Leader:** _BOSS_, _(party\_size+dungeon\_level)d6_ HP, and produces _(dungeon\_level+1)d6_ damage. This ruthless opponent has _(dungeon\_level+party\_size/3)_ orc minions by his side. These minions are normal Orcs that have _1_ HP and do _(dungeon\_level+1)+1d6_ damage.

**Armored:** The Orc Leader has _(dungeon\_level+1)_ AC.

_Upon reaching the surface:_ You place the leader's tusks on the counter and people fall silent. You are paid _(character\_level*350)_ g each, and receive recognition as a champion. You have leveled up.

## Rescue An NPC

From kidnapped villagers to unwise adventurers, you may find yourself involved in a rescue operation. While the specifics lie with the person in question, in general the idea is to go in, find the person, then reach the surface with said person in good health. Failing to protect the person means failing the quest.

Roll a _d6_ to find out who needs your assistance to find its way out:

### NPC Table

### NPC Rescue 1 - The Lost Archaeologist

You are tasked with retrieving an archaeologist. Apparently the archaologist wandered off during a research expedition. The clerk says in a relaxed tone:

> As you know, there are so many wondrous things in the depths and academics are anything but reasonable. Just bring that bookworm back in one piece.

First reach your target _Dungeon Level_, where the Lost Archeologist was last seen. Then continue exploring until you roll a _Quest Event_.

_Quest Event:_ You find the archaeologist. The Archaeologist however, is frail of health and can't keep up with the party. You must slow down your pace, the party will expend _2_ non combat events each time you travel to a visited location (including the stairs) for the purposes of hazard die rolls (if you have a _Diseased_ or _Poisoned_ member, this handicap does not increase).

_Upon reaching the surface:_ The archaeologist is very grateful and says that you will be commended for your efforts though unfortunately there's no financial reward. 3 months later you receive a letter of recommendation from the academy for a low skill position as a grounds keeper. You shrug and toss it aside. You managed to level up.

### NPC Rescue 2 - The Missing Child

You enter the guild, a couple are sobbing next to the clerk. The clerk turns and asks that you retrieve their child. In a somber tone she adds:

> A lost dog, and a lack of wisdom are all you need for a child to loose its bearings inside the bowels of the dungeon. Surprisingly, the child has a talisman that has kept the horrors at bay but it won't hold for much longer. So make the rescue before we have a casualty.

Follow the track of this child until you reach your target _Dungeon Level_, then a _Quest Event_ roll will advance the story further.

_Quest Event:_ You found the child. This child is afraid of vermin, if you're attacked by spiders, or bats it will run away and need to be found in that level again in a visited room. If the child runs away see searching for the child.

_Searching for the child:_ Enter a room and roll a _d6_, you find the child on a _5_ or _6_. If you find the child, ignore the _Hazard Die_ roll for that one room. Don't immediately search in a room you already searched.

_Upon reaching the surface:_ Two parents in tears greet you at the entrance, you're each paid _(character\_level*5)_ g and level up. Not all rewards are financial in this world. You managed to level up.

### NPC Rescue 3 - Endangered Old Timer

The clerk looks at you begins talking as she sorts some papers:

> Some people can't come to terms with the passing of time, and so this old timer took its gear and went in. Nobody would bat an eye if the will were not stored in that armor, hence the pious second child who would not inherit has ordered you to return that old timer alive if possible and that will at all costs.

Descend to your target _Dungeon Level_, the quest will advance when you roll your next Quest Event.

_Quest Event:_ You find the old timer who turns out to be much stronger than any of you, has _(party\_size+2\*character\_level)d6_ HP more than your party and is able to attack using _2\*(character\_level)d6_ while casting Restore Vitality once per fight, and is capable of double attacks. However, his blasted helmet has a foul charm that forces you to throw a Hazard die every tile for every revisited room (essentially moving equates to 4 non combat events). And, only has _(dungeon\_level+1)d6_ rations. Not to mention, he counts as a party member for all intents and purposes meaning all your encounters will be tougher.

<div class="page"/>

_Upon reaching the surface:_ The old timer invites you to his home for a pint and rest near the fireplace where the old timer throws the will. The next morning, you're offered _(200*character\_level)_ g each. You managed to level up.

### NPC Rescue 4 - The Missing Alchemist

You are taken to a separate room where the guild master describes a delicate situation:

>It seems that a well known alchemist has decided wander off on its own into the depths of the dungeon and there are concerns, financial and influential concerns, about the well being of said alchemist. You're tasked with easing those concerns by retrieving the lost researcher.

Make your way down to your target _Dungeon Level_ then keep an eye out for your next quest event.

_Quest Event:_ Crying in a corner like a lost child is the alchemist. The alchemist says in a wobbly voice: "I failed because I succeeded, I'm a fool because I'm a genius, what a sad day this triumph is my worst defeat ever". After a few more contrasting praises and derision, he explains that he succeeded in creating a Golem that can be disassembled but not destroyed and it won't let him out.

The Golem will be chasing you on your way out, all the way to the entrance (luckily, it's bound to the dungeon).

<div class="page"/>

Instead of a Hazard dice, roll to see the if Golem has found you:

| Roll | Outcome |
| ---- | ------- |
| 5-6 | Thankfully it was just your nerves, best to keep moving. |
| 3-4 | The Golem is behind you, you can engage or attempt to flee. |
| 1-2 | The Golem is in front of you, no chance to escape you will have to fight. |

The Golem is very different to most encounters. First it will withstand _(dungeon\_level+1)*2_ effective attacks, the damage dealt does not matter only the number of attacks. Missed attacks do not count against this total. Also the Golem is immune to any tactics, items or spells. Each impact does the same amount of damage, bypassing any armor. Additionally, when struck by the Golem roll a _Magic Save_ of _(dungeon\_level+1)_ (using the dungeon\_level where Golem was initially found) or be paralyzed until combat concludes. The damage it does and difficulty of the saves depends on the level it was initially found and does not decrease as you approach the surface.

<div class="page"/>

_Escape is generally discouraged, but may be worth considering in this case. Hence, as a reminder, to escape from the Golem all characters must roll a successful Dex Save. If any one character fails the save, escape fails and the Golem will attack first._

Upon defeat, the Golem remains immobile until the next _Hazard Die_ roll (it malfunctions temporarily, but resumes operating shortly).

_Upon reaching the surface:_ The guards are ready to apprehend the alchemist, when a very ornate chariot from the treasury arrives to take the alchemist away. You are handed _(1000*character\_level)_ g each then told you did not witness anything. You managed to level up.

After the disappearance of his sister, the child was questioned and is reported to have said: "I never thought she'd fall for it". The family of this young lady has requested your services, her fiance says she can stay in there for all he cares. So, it's now time to put chivalry to the test and get this unfortunate back to the surface.

Well so long as the pay is good, it doesn't really matter.

Once you reach the target _Dungeon Level_, keep an eye out for a _Quest Event_ roll.

_Quest Event:_ You find the Damsel, but maybe it's your imagination but something seems unusual about this. Roll a _d6_...

<div class="page"/>

| ---- | ------- |

_Upon reaching the surface:_

_If she's still alive:_ the family takes her away violently and with a look of disgust throws each of you a sack of gems worth _(500*character\_level)_ g. You managed to level up.

_If she was turned into a vampire:_ The family refuses your claims despite the evidence and their influence leaves the guild with no recourse but consider your mission failed. The guild master apologizes but mentions how not doing so would create a lot of problems for the guild. You're still offered a pint and a chance to rest. But you didn't level up or earn any money, this makes the ale taste rather bitter.

Guess it's always good to be able to be of help even to those far stronger than yourself. Maybe there's a place for all adventurers.

### Treasure  Raid 1 - Retrieve The Green Armor

The clerk coughs briefly then reads:

> Runes have been found pointing to the legendary green armor used by the ancient dwarfs in the battle against the shadow fiends. There are people in high places with interest in this relic, you will be provided researcher notes to decipher the clues to locate and extract said Green Armor.

You are handed the notes, and 2 sets of thieves tools for each then dismissed.

<div class="page"/>

First descend to the appropriate level, then the quests events will take place - you must find clues, then decipher them to make your way to the armor.

_Quest Event1:_ You encounter a glyph in the wall, roll a _Magic Save_ to successfully decipher the glyphs.
_On Success:_ The arrows seem to be pointing you in a definite direction, move to Quest event 2.
_On Failure:_ These glyphs are too vague, you must search for more clues before having a definite answer. Repeat Quest Event 1.

_Quest Event2:_ The glyphs told you about a secret passage, and how to understand it. Travel to a room with an unobstructed secret door, then roll a _Detect Secret_ to discover the passage.
_On Success:_ This passage is exactly as described in the glyphs you're on the right track!
_On Failure:_ This can't be it, must keep searching. Continue looking for a room with an unobstructed secret passage.

_Quest Event3:_ You have finally found it, the legendary green armor. But... something's wrong, there's a weird shadow that keeps moving on top of it. A shadow creature wearing the armor rises and attacks!

<div class="page"/>

_Armored Shadow creature:_ _(party\_size+dungeon\_level)d6_ HP immune to _Sneak Attacks_ and _Sedation_, _+3_ AC. While it lacks a blade, it flails its arms and is able to attack proficiently attacking as if it had a single handed weapon causing _(dungeon\_level)d6_ HP damage.

**Undead:** The Armored Shadow is an undead creature.

_This creature is vulnerable to Aura of Light and Illumination spells, they make it unable to hold on to the armor (it looses its 3 AC)._

Once defeated, you hold the mythical green armor. It's a +_(3+dungeon\_level)_ AC unique armor, that molds to the body shape of the wearer and is extremely light (anybody can use it). You're free to wear it on the way back. So time to make your way to the surface.

_Upon reaching the surface:_ It's tempting to hold on to the armor, however if you decided to keep it you'd have fight of the guild and perhaps the entire army so suffice to say once you hand it over to the guild who themselves hand it over to a rather scrawny noble who'll never see combat in his lifetime. Still you're rewarded _(200*character\_level)_ g each, and leveled up. Could be worse.

A short and slim yet stern looking lady places a stool to make eye contact then says in a clear tone:

> There are rumors of a small lizard being worshiped as a deity. It's your job to figure out if that's a threat, if there's treasure  and if so - in the name of the crown - seize it and bring it to the surface.

First descend to the appropriate level, then the quest events will take place.

You will face Quest Encounter 1 until you successfully finish Quest Event 1.

_Quest Encounter 1_: _(party\_size)_ Normal Kobolds

_Normal Kobold_: _(dungeon\_level+1)d6_, inflicts _(dungeon\_level+1)_ damage, but on a _Critical Attack_, in addition to its attack, it will throw a tiny spider. It's identical to the spider but it only has 1 HP.

_Quest Event1:_ You see a roasted rodent on the ground. There could be some truth to the claims... move on to Quest event 2, also you will face Quest Encounter 2 during Quest Encounters.

_Quest Encounter 2_: _(party\_size)_ Kobold Warriors.

_Kobold Warrior_: _(dungeon\_level+2)d6_ HP, inflicts _(dungeon\_level)d6_ damage. On any _Critical Attack_, in addition to its attack, it will throw a small spider. It's identical to the spider but it has _(2+dungeon\_level)_ HP.

<div class="page"/>

_Quest Event2:_ You hear a high pitched scream:

> "I am master of thunder, me must be feared!"

Followed by a storm from within the walls. This can't be good!

After fulfilling Quest Event 2, the next Quest event is Quest Event 3 however you may also face the quest encounter without activating that event!

_Quest Encounter 3_: Kobold Thunder Wielder.

_Kobold Thunder Wielder_: (party/2+dungeon\_level)d6, its dagger causes _(dungeon\_level+1)d6_ damage to everyone in the party at once, _Magic Save_ for half damage. Additionally, on a _Near Critical Attack_ a minion kobold joins him. Minion Kobolds are identical to normal kobolds but they have only _1_ hp. However if the mirror shield was discovered, the lightning bounces back, shocking the kobold who releases the dagger and runs away.

_Quest Event3:_ You see an adventurer, his head charred, but his arm intact. He seems to be wearing a very reflective shield. You decide to take the shield.

_After defeating the kobold:_ You take the dagger from its cold dead hands. Take a deep breath and regret doing so, my gosh are these nasty creatures stinky. Without too much fanfare, you begin your journey to the surface. You will no longer face opponents in your next encounter.

_If the kobold ran away:_ You pickup the dagger, and begin the process of heading to the surface. Unfortunately, Kobolds are very vengeful and so for each quest event you will be fighting Kobold warriors until you make it out of the dungeon.

The dagger does _(dungeon\_level+1)d6_ to any opponents, and you can use it on your way up.

_Upon reaching the surface:_ As you enter the guild people turn up their noses in disgust, with a grin you place the stinky dagger on the counter. It's carried off in a handkerchief that's irredeemably sullied upon contact. You're rewarded _(25*character\_level)_ g each, you argue about it, but the guild claims that they had to spend the rest in removing the stench from the counter. You managed to level up.

### Treasure  Raid 3 - Locate And Retrieve The Ancient Orb

A scruffy looking man hands a crumpled note then walks away without missing a step...

The grand magus herself has sent a request to the guild (not in person, that's for lesser magicians) for the retrieval of the ancient orb. Being as wise as she is, she does not know that the rest of the world is not as erudite. You ask around but hear nothing. With absolutely no clues, you enter the dungeon.

Soon you start thinking: Just because nobody knows about the orb in the surface doesn't mean nobody knows about it below the surface, though who could I ask around here?

First travel to the appropriate level (stairs count in this case), then visit rooms until you roll a _Quest Event_, or manage to convince a friendly monster into helping you go to _Quest Event 1_.

_Quest Event1:_ (same if you successfully pay or convince a monster to tell you) You receive a lead regarding an ancient relic for magicians. You will now look for clues in the glyphs. Go to _Quest Event 2_.

_Quest Event2:_ You carefully study the ornaments on the walls and realize that said orb is here, roll a _Magic Save_ to determine if you can expedite the search. On success, search for a secret door then go to _Quest Event 4_, on failure, go to _Quest Event 3_.

<div class="page"/>

_Quest Event3:_ You study the walls and finally it all makes sense, the answer was not as complex as you thought (but upon reflecting on it, none of it makes sense so you stop reflecting on it). You search for a secret door, then go to _Quest Event 4_.

_Quest Event4:_ You study the glyphs and soon hear a voice in your head inviting you somewhere. Unconsciously you begin walking towards the wall, and soon you're in a beautiful garden with springs and wonderful clear skies. An orb is placed on a pillar: "You can take me with you, but first you must solve a riddle" images flash in your mind, they come fast and are far too complex for you to clearly identify anything let alone articulate what you saw. "So what is your answer?" Says the voice. You are stumped, this is like asking a 2 year old child about the finances of the kingdom or advanced military tactics. Roll a _Magic Save_:
_On Success:_ You manage to bluff your way through, and take the orb. You don't really know what the orb is or does, you don't know what you were asked, but you do know you're 1 _(dungeon\_level)_ above the level you entered (unless you were in ground level in which case you return to ground level). Now it's time to make your way back.
_on failure:_ The orb disappears and you're in ground level next to the entrance.

<div class="page"/>

_Upon reaching the surface:_
_If you have the Orb:_ You receive _(1000*character\_level)_ g each, and soon an invitation to the royal academy of magic is in your hands. You try to open the letter and realize that it won't budge, and the letter just escapes your fingers an leaves... Good, it's not like you needed the aggravation anyway you tell yourself (someone seems to be cutting onions nearby). Confused, you realize that the only solution is at the bottom of a large mug. You managed to level up.

_If you don't have the Orb:_ You try to explain what happened and the clerk makes a silence gesture when a violent flash illuminates the sky followed by a deafening roar and a very loud and sustained ringing in your ears. Seems the magus is not pleased. You have failed the quest, you did not manage to level up but, you did manage to go to the pub.

### Treasure  Raid 4 - Retrieve The Wisdom Amulet

A sleepy clerk clears its throat and says after a deep yawn:

First travel to the appropriate level; then, visit rooms until you trigger a quest event.

_Quest Event1:_ You find said glyphs and with no difficulty realize where it leads. Search for a secret door, then upon discovering the door unlock to go to quest event 2. Failure to unlock or find the secret door means that was not the room and you must keep searching.

_Quest Event2:_ These symbols conflict with all you were told, the bishop is lying, this is a trap (roll a _Dex Save_ _On Success_ you anticipate the trap on failure roll _Trap_ then keep reading). These glyphs seem to give a warning rather than invitation. In any case, the way forward seems to be through a secret entrance. Search for an unobstructed secret door then instead of entering it, or activating another event go directly to _Quest Event 3_.

<div class="page"/>

_Quest Event3:_ You notice a gem protruding from the glyphs on the wall, and two hands below it. You place 6 days rations (if you don't have them you must procure to advance - go look for rations) then press on the gem press it. The wall shudders then moves to reveal a pedestal with the pendant placed on top. Next to it are two statues, one them is a muscular man in an athletic posture, while the other is a wise man lost deep in thought.

To achieve wisdom you must attain balance... Whom among you is worthy?

1. First choose a party member.
2. Take the numbers on your saves and from your and write that down elsewhere. If you chose an Elf, then subtract one from Magic and Dex, and one to Body. If you chose Dwarf subtract two from Body and add one to Dex. Humans are unaffected.
3. Roll _3d6_.
4. Assign a dice to each save so that the value in your die is greater than the save. If you chose a human and you haven't used your _Dice Re-Roll_, you can do so now.
5. If all three saves were successful, you have achieved balance. Otherwise, you have failed.

<div class="page"/>

_On success:_ You take the wisdom pendant and put it on, you concentrate and visualize yourself brething deeply and evenly. You realize that you were right... Not much insight there. You concentrate harder, and regularize your breath, and come to the realization that you should make your way back to the surface. Does this pendant do anything at all?
_On failure:_ You reach for the wisdom pendant when the statues come to life and attack!

_athletic statue:_ _(dungeon\_level+3)_ HP, immune to _Sneak Attack_ and _Sedation_. When hit, roll a _Body Save_ or be stunned for two rounds.
_thinking statue:_ _(dungeon\_level)_ HP,  immune to _Sneak Attack_ and _Sedation_. Casts _Sleep_ on the party, roll a _Magic Save_ or sleep for _(dungeon\_level)_ rounds - no sleep on ground level.

_Upon defeating the statues:_ The talisman shatters.

Time to head for the surface, if you roll another quest event the pendant activates and you have an inner monolog (optional)

_Quest Event4:_ You realize that this pendant will probably be far less useful to most people than they thought. It's not useless per se, but it won't do the work for them which is what people expect out of it, instead it will point out what they already know to be correct.

<div class="page"/>

_Quest Event5:_ Many ideas come to mind, you begin to see your place in the world clearly...

_Upon reaching the surface:_
_If you're wearing the talisman:_ You notice the clerk is about to hand you a fraction of what you deserve, pocketing the rest of it. You smile and tell the clerk that you can wait and will come when the guild master is ready to collect. The clerk seems irritated but hands over the bag stored behind the counter with the rest of your earrings _(350*character\_level)_ g each,. You start thinking about your profession, are you really on the right path? And then you start chocking as the clerk pulls on the necklace - you hand it over. You realize how dumb this whole ordeal was, but you had enough savviness to get the most out things... Then the next thing you know you have already drunk enough for 10 and are working towards making that 20, guess the necklace's effect is non-permanent.
_If it shattered:_ You are questioned and treated almost like a traitor to the kingdom. Everyone thinks you stole the necklace, eventually you are released but get no money. You were not able to level up.

### Treasure  Raid 5 - Obtain The Night Cloak

You receive a commission from an undisclosed source to retrieve the "Night Cloak". No particular details of the artifact are revealed, but there is an urgency to this. You are provided some clues as to how to decipher its location, and are told that there is a keen interest in this particular item. Soon you set off...

You enter the dungeon and get a feeling someone might be watching you, it's a bit unusual to be honest and not quite comforting.

This is a special quest, from ground level until you make your way to the target level, all quests encounters and quest events trigger an encounter, roll to determine which:

#### Night Cloak Encounter Table

1-2. _Ruffian Mage:_ _(dungeon\_level)d6_ HP, casts Fireball and Stop once each.
3-4. _Ruffian Rogue:_ _(dungeon\_level)d6_ HP, the character lowest on HP must roll a _Dex Save_ or receive _6+(1d6)_ damage.
5-6. _Ruffian Fighter:_ _(dungeon\_level)d6_ HP, attacks the strongest in the party first. On landing a _Critical Attack_ it will perform a second attack, that could land further attacks on successive crits.
