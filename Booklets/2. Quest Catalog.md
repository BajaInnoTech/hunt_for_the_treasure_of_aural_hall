### Quest 1 - Hunt Down The Orc Leader

An particularly fearless and fierce orc has been rallying troops with the intention to take over the settlement. You're tasked with hunting him down before he raises a sufficiently sized army to attack the surface. The clerk finally gives you a warning:

> Unfortunately, there are no further reports on this suspect. No known weaknesses that could be exploited, so be on your toes for this one!

You crack your knuckles and head to the entrance.

_In this quest, quest encounters take place starting at level 0. Once you have reached the appropriate level, the quest encounter triggers a boss fight._

_Quest Encounter_ - You find a garrison of orcs, you fight _(1+(party_size)/2)_ orcs with _(dungeon_level+1)_ HP

**Orc Leader:** _BOSS_, _(party_size+dungeon_level)d6_ HP, and produces _(dungeon_level+1)d6_ damage. This ruthless opponent has _(dungeon_level+party_size/3)_ orc minions by his side. These minions are normal Orcs that have _1_ HP and do _(dungeon_level+1)+1d6_ damage.

**Armored:** The Orc leader has _(dungeon_level+1)_ AC.

_Upon reaching the surface:_ You place the leader's tusks on the counter and people fall silent. You are paid _(350*character_level)_ g each, and receive recognition as a champion. You have leveled up.

## Rescue An NPC

From kidnapped villagers to unwise adventurers, you may find yourself involved in a rescue operation. While the specifics lie with the person in question, in general the idea is to go in, find the person, then reach the surface with said person in good health. Failing to protect the person means failing the quest.

Roll a d6 to find out who needs your assistance to find its way out:

### NPC Rescue 1 - Archaeologist

You are tasked with retrieving an archaeologist. Apparently the archaologist wandered off during a research expedition. The clerk says in a relaxed tone:

> As you know, there are so many wondrous things in the depths and academics are often not very good at being reasonable. Just bring that bookworm back in one piece.

_Quest Event:_ You find the archaeologist. The Archaeologist however, is frail of health and can't keep up with the party. You must slow down your pace, the party will expend _2_ non combat events each time you travel to a visited location (including the stairs) for the purposes of hazard die rolls (if you have a diseased or poisoned member, this handicap does not increase).

_Upon reaching the surface:_ The archaeologist is very grateful and says that you will be commended for your efforts though unfortunately there's no financial reward. 3 months later you receive a letter of recommendation from the academy for a low skill position as a grounds keeper. You shrug and toss it aside. You managed to level up.

### NPC Rescue 2 - Child

You enter the guild, a couple are sobbing next to the clerk. The clerk turns and asks that you retrieve their child. In a somber tone she adds:

> A lost dog, and a lack of wisdom are all you need for a child to loose its bearings inside the bowels of the dungeon. Surprisingly, the child has a talisman that has kept the horrors at bay but it won't hold for much longer. So make the rescue before we have a casualty.

_Quest Event:_ You found the child. This child is afraid of vermin, if you're attacked by spiders, or bats it will run away and need to be found in that level again in a visited room. If the child runs away see searching for the child.

_Searching for the child:_ Enter a room and roll a d6, you find the child on a _5_ or _6_. If you find the child, ignore the hazard die roll for that one room. Don't immediately search in a room you already searched.

_Upon reaching the surface:_ Two parents in tears greet you at the entrance, you're paid _5_ g and level up. Not all rewards are financial in this world. You managed to level up.

### NPC Rescue 4 - Alchemist

You are taken to a separate room where the guild master describes a delicate situation. It seems that a well known alchemist has decided wander on its own into the depths of the dungeon and there are concerns, financial and influential concerns, about the well being of said alchemist. You're tasked with easing those concerns by retrieving the lost researcher.

_Quest Event:_ Crying in a corner like a lost child is the alchemist. The alchemist says in a wobbly voice: "I failed because I succeeded, I'm a fool because I'm a genius, what a sad day this triumph is my worst defeat ever". After a few more contrasting praises and derision, he explains that he succeeded in creating a Golem that can be disassembled but not destroyed and it won't let him out.

The Golem will be chasing you on your way out, all the way to the entrance (luckily, it's bound to the dungeon).

Instead of a Hazard dice, roll to see the if Golem has found you:

| Roll | Outcome |
| ---- | ------- |
| 5-6 | Thankfully it was just your nerves, best to keep moving. |
| 3-4 | The Golem is behind you, you can engage or attempt to flee. |
| 1-2 | The Golem is in front of you, no chance to escape you will have to fight. |

The Golem is very different to most encounters. First it will withstand _(dungeon_level+1)*2_ effective attacks, the damage dealt does not matter only the number of attacks. Missed attacks do not count against this total. Also the Golem is immune to any tactics, items or spells. Each impact does the same amount of damage, bypassing any armor. Additionally, when struck by the Golem roll a save against magic of _(dungeon_level+1)_ (using the dungeon_level where Golem was initially found) or be paralyzed until combat concludes. The damage it does and difficulty of the saves depends on the level it was initially found and does not decrease as you approach the surface.

_Escape is generally discouraged, but may be worth considering in this case. Hence, as a reminder, to escape from the Golem all characters must roll a successful dex save. If any one character fails the save, escape fails and the Golem will attack first._

Upon defeat, the Golem remains immobile until the next hazard die roll (it malfunctions temporarily, but resumes operating shortly).

_Upon reaching the surface:_ The guards are ready to apprehend the alchemist, when a very ornate chariot from the treasury arrives to take the alchemist away. You are handed _(1000*character_level)_ g and told you did not witness anything. You managed to level up.

### Treasure  Raid 1 - Retrieve The Green Armor

Runes have been found pointing to the legendary green armor used by the ancient dwarfs in the battle against the shadow fiends.

First descend to the appropriate level, then the quests events will take place - you will find clues, and decipher them to discover the armor.

_Quest Event1:_ You encounter a glyph in the wall, roll a magic save to successfully decipher the glyphs.
_On Success:_ The arrows seem to be pointing you in a definite direction, move to Quest event 2.
_On Failure:_ These glyphs are too vague, you must search for more clues before having a definite answer. Repeat Quest Event 1.

_Quest Event2:_ The glyphs told you about a secret passage, and how to understand it. Travel to a room with an unobstructed secret door, then roll to discover the passage.
_On Success:_ This passage is exactly as described in the glyphs you're on the right track!
_On Failure:_ This can't be it, must keep searching. Continue looking for a room with an unobstructed secret passage.

_Quest Event3:_ You have finally found it, the legendary green armor. But... something's wrong, there's a weird shadow that keeps moving on top of it. A shadow creature wearing the armor rises and attacks!

_Armored Shadow creature:_ _(party_size+dungeon_level)d6_ HP immune to sneak attacks and sedation, _+3_ AC. While it lacks a blade, it flails its arms and is able to attack proficiently attacking as if it had a single handed weapon causing _(dungeon_level)d6_ HP damage.

**Undead:** The Armored Shadow is an undead creature.

_This creature is vulnerable to Aura of Light and Illumination spells, they make it unable to hold on to the armor (it looses its 3 AC)._

Once defeated, you hold the mythical green armor. It's a +_(3+dungeon_level)_ AC unique armor, that molds to the body shape of the wearer and is extremely light (anybody can use it). You're free to wear it on the way back. So time to make your way to the surface.

_Upon reaching the surface:_ It's tempting to hold on to the armor, however if you decided to keep it you'd have fight of the guild and perhaps the entire army so suffice to say once you hand it over to the guild who themselves hand it over to a rather scrawny noble who'll never see combat in his lifetime. Still you're rewarded _(100*character\_level)_ g each, and leveled up. Could be worse.

#### The Shrine Of The Night Cloak

You enter the shrine, there are 4 statues illuminating an empty pedestal. What on earth could this be about? Roll a d6:

1. The statues must be toppled by might, roll a body save for each statue. On success you move to the next, on failure you take 3HP damage from the debris that fell off. Upon success, you obtain cloak of might, then head back to the surface.

<div class="page"/>

2. The statues must be toppled by skill, roll a dex save for each statue. On success, you climb on a ledge and make your way to the top of the statue and detach the source of light. On failure you take 2HP damage because you fell performing said maneuver. Upon success, you obtain the cloak of the venturer then head back to the surface.
3. The statues must be toppled by intellect, roll a magic save for each statue. On success, you move to the next, on failure you take 2HP psychic damage from the statue. Upon success, you obtain the cloak of the archmage then head back to the surface.
4. The cape requires a bit of slight of hand to obtain. You tie rope around a weapon and make a dex save to hit the shinny part of the statue with a -1 penalty, you don't take any damage but it sure isn't easy. After 3 failures on a statue, roll the dice again for another option on this table. On success, you obtain the cloak of skulduggery then head back to the surface.
5. You reach out directly to the pedestal and run your fingers on it, you feel soft cloth and then a sharp pain. You get 1 HP damage, the cloth falls to the ground and a mechanical automaton attacks. After defeating it, you obtain the cloak of misdirection then head back to the surface.
6. You pull your resources together, coordinating your efforts on each statue. Roll a save vs magic, body and dex. On success the moment a statue is disabled the cloak appears, and you obtain cloak of concealment then head back to the surface.

_Mechanical Automaton:_ (1d6) HP immune to sneak attack and sedation, requires a roll of 2 or greater to hit and only takes 1 HP of damage regardless of the attack.

Cloak variants:

_Cloak of might:_ The wearer has 4 additional d6 of damage.
_Cloak of the venturer:_ The wearer is capable of dodging any attack not on a 6.
_Cloak of the archmage:_ The wearer can cast fireball nonstop.
_Cloak of skulduggery:_ The wearer always performs a sneak attack even if the target is an undead, and even if the wearer is not a Rogue. Rogues perform a sneak attack on every attack.
_Cloak of misdirection:_ The wearer can confuse the enemy, on a roll of 3 or greater, the enemy will see the wearer as one of its kin and ignore them.
_Cloak of concealment:_ This cloak looks more like a bed sheet. You see the stars drawn on one end and nothing but pitch black on the other, as light were being devoured. If the party remains close, most enemies will not be able to find them (for every encounter, roll a d6 on a 3 or more you manage to avoid the conflict).

<div class="page"/>

_Upon reaching the surface:_ The clerk takes the cloak and tosses it aside like rotten cabbage hands you 10g each and brushes you off, then a mysterious and unsavory character grabs the poor clerk by the throat and tosses him with the same disdain. This individual wears the cloak and suddenly it comes to life. You can see multiple worlds moving from one part to another, then suddenly the cloak turns a dull gray. You receive _(1500*character\_level)_ g each, then the stranger disappears before your eyes. You managed to level up.

### Treasure  Raid 6 - Capture And Return The Cursed Fang

The archbishop himself is standing right next to the clerk and turns towards you, handing you a letter then waves you off. The letter says:

> The cursed fang is a vestige from the land of shadows. It pulsates with necrotic energy and could snuff the life of a large city in the right hands. Hence it must be captured and returned

There are some sketches on it that should help narrow its location.

This is a special quest, from ground level until you make your way to the target level, all quests encounters and quest events trigger an encounter, roll to determine which:

#### Cursed Fang Encounter Table

1-2. _Lesser Vampire_
3-4. _Zombies_
5-6. _Skeleton_

Upon reaching the appropriate level, only quest encounters trigger an encounter table roll. Visit rooms until you trigger a quest event.

<div class="page"/>

_Quest Event 1:_ You see an ominous mark, roll a magic save to see if you can decipher the contents.
_On Success:_ The scribbling depicts the events of a terrible battle where the creatures of shadow attempted to conquer the kingdom. At the center of this is a shiny gem that is not only worth a fortune but is crudely out of place. You could either try to pry it open or attempt to block in some way...

* _Pry it open:_ You choose to pry it open, roll a dex save-1 (you need one more point than usual), if you succeed you will also expend 2 lock picks (you use them in an unconventional manner and spoil them). On success: the jewel is in your hand, go to on success. On failure: You can attempt again or choose another strategy.
* _Darken it with magic_: You cast Invisibility on the rock itself, the rock fades - go to on success.

_On success:_ A mechanism activates at a distance, next time trigger quest event 2.

_Quest Event 2:_ The glyphs on the wall remind you of what you saw last time, this time however a jewel is missing where it should be (you're getting good at this, you got it without any effort). There are three ways in which you could solve this:

* If you took the jewel from the previous glyph, place the very expensive jewel from the other glyph: The jewel fits neatly, you hear a click at a distance... sadly you will have to part ways with the loot. Go to On Success.
* Use Illuminate spell: You focus your attention (roll a d6, if you have 2 or more you succeed) then place a light in place of the missing jewel, the wall shudders but won't budge. There's an orifice just a small nudge expending thieves tools will do it (you use a set of thieves tools). You hear a click in the distance, go to on success. If you don't have thieves tools, you unfortunately go to on failure.
* Using a torch and thieves tools: Sometimes you must take the hard road to get somewhere... To make this happen you expend a torch and a set of thieves tools, in addition you must perform a successful lock-picking, detect secrets and magic save. If you succeed then go to on success, otherwise go to on failure.

_On Failure:_ Seems that this won't budge. Perhaps it would be a good idea to try and pry that jewel go to Quest Event 1.

_On Success:_ This is it, you're sure to have unlocked the path! Now to find an unobstructed secret passage, when that happens trigger the third Quest Event automatically (no need for an event roll this time).

_Quest Event 3:_ You detect a hidden pathway and begin looking for clues when you see the door open wide, a nasty Troll comes out of it. From its neck hangs the _Cursed Fang_, this brute turned it into a necklace.

_Troll:_ (party_size+dungeon_level)d6, regenarates 2HP each round and causes (dungeon_level+1)d6 harm. Though it's been said trolls are vulnerable to fire (double damage).

_Upon defeating the troll:_ You take a part of its skirt and wrap the evil item in it, then use the necklace part to fasten it to your belt. Best to go back now.

_Cursed fang:_ With the cursed fang in your possession, all undead encounters are dismissed. They simply ignore your party as if you weren't there.

_Upon reaching the surface:_ The archbishop himself is at the exit of the dungeon. Whatever this thing really is must be important. Two high priests place the item in a sealed container, you receive _(3000*character\_level)_ g each, and leveled up.

### Quest 4 - Exchange Prisoner Elgar

You receive a letter from the captain of the guard:

> Report to the Central Prison for a prisoner transfer, details upon arrival. Be sure to equip yourselves with extra rations.

Upon arriving at the prison, you are directed to the appropriate cell by a guard, the cellar is standing next to the door smiling. You receive a series of indications that describe what you need to do and where you need to go. The prisoner is bound by chains in hands and feet, you are given _1d6_ bandages (roll to determine how many), then the guard tells you:

> You will deliver someone and retrieve someone, both of which wish to escape, and that can be a big problem if they do. Since you won't be allowed back out or at the drop off location until you find your inmate. Also, you are tasked with keeping the prisoner alive, if the prisoner escapes, DO NOT kill it during recapture! I repeat, you must make sure the prisoner does not escape or die during transit. Cellar if you'd do the honors...

<div class="page"/>

The cellar clears his throat and puts the key in the cell door. Then the guard continues:

> You have the privilege of escorting Half-Elf Elgar. He's wanted for high treason here, but he's equally wanted for high treason in his homeland. However, they have a dark magus who needs to be interrogated and the cellar is only too eager to provide assistance (yeesh, some things you rather not know about). The job is to take him to this location...

Prisoner you will deliver:
_Elgar:_ _2d6_ HP, Elf Rogue.

Prisoner you will receive in exchange:
_Morton:_ _(2d6-dungeon_level)_ (at least _3_), Human Mage. Knows Stop and Invisibility.

> Pleased to meet your acquaintance!

Says Elgar sarcastically! You brush it off and make your way to the entrance.

**Recapture Mechanism:** Should the prisoner escape, during recapture Elgar will inflict _1d6_ damage each round. To recapture Elgar, you must roll a 5 or 6 to incapacitate - he'll receive 1HP damage. If you roll a 1, then you will inflict _1d6_ damage and the prisoner will escape injured. You have two roundsj during recapture before he escapes, in which case you must locate the prisoner again and begin the process anew.

<div class="page"/>

**Hazard die rolls:** If you're revisiting rooms, the prisoner will delay your efforts to reach your destination. The party will expend 2 non combat events each time you travel to a visited location (including the stairs). Should the inmate die, you will carry the corpse which slows you even further so you'll have to throw a hazard die every room. In which case, perhaps you could revive the prisoner, at least before handing him over?

**Traps and inmate health:** Traps that are set off will also affect the inmate, so be careful!

**Rest and lunch breaks:** Since you have a prisoner who wishes to escape, you can't rest until you have delivered the exchanged prisoner. This includes eating at the staircase.

_Quest Events while escorting Elgar:_ The prisoner attempts to escape, roll a dex save to stop the prisoner in time. If the Prisoner has escaped, ignore these events until he's captured.

_Quest Encounters while Escorting Elgar:_ If the Prisoner is in custody ignore the encounters, otherwise you find the prisoner.

Upon reaching the level, you look for an unobstructed secret door and roll a magic save. If you succeed, you notice a keyhole. Roll a lock-picking check if you succeed again, go to on success.

<div class="page"/>

_On Success:_ You open the door to meet a series of armed guards at the ready. They all look elven, but their skin is dark. The one at the front motions you to drop your weapons to the floor.

_If you have the prisoner - alive:_ One of the guards pulls on Elgar's chains then uses the momentum to ram the hilt of his sword straight onto his jaw splattering blood and pieces of teeth on the floor. With an effortless motion he proceeds to carry him unconscious. The rest of the guards put their weapons to your throats but remain otherwise immobile. After a moderate wait period, you see a very beaten up man. In addition to manacles, he's also gagged. Time to make your way back.
_If you have the prisoner - dead:_ The front guard collects the body, pulling it by the hair and tossing it to the other guards. You are given a letter formally refusing the exchange. You must make your way back.
_If you don't have the prisoner:_ The front guard collects your weapons then sends an arrow straight onto your shoulder, you suffer 1d6HP damage. The wall then closes - The message is clear, find the prisoner before returning! What a day...

_On your way back:_ Much like Elgar, Morton does not wish to arrive to his destination, and will put up a much greater fight.

<div class="page"/>

_Quest Events while escorting Morton:_ The prisoner attempts to escape his gag, roll a dex save to stop the prisoner in time. If the Prisoner manages to escape the gag he will immediately cast Stop on you and become invisible then run away. Morton has escaped, ignore further events until he's captured again.

_Quest Encounters while Escorting Morton:_ If the Prisoner is in custody ignore the encounters, otherwise you find the prisoner. Morton inflicts 2 damage, you must roll a _5_ or _6_ to incapacitate him, but he'll receive _1_ HP damage. On a 1 you inflict _1d6_ damage and he escapes injured (if he survives). You have one round to capture him before he turn invisible and escapes.

_Upon reaching the surface:_
_If you deliver Morton alive:_ You finally reach the prison. The cellar greets you at the entrance with a small garrison. He thanks you for your efforts and the many happy nights ahead, meanwhile you can see Morton's pale complexion upon hearing about this... Seems the cellar has quite a reputation! You return to the guild and receive _(300*character_level)_ g each, and an invitation to the party later that night by the cellar. While it's always nice for people to remember you fondly, you decide you rather party with a tankard full of ale and forget this incident. You managed to level up.

<div class="page"/>

_If you deliver Morton dead:_ You reach a security outpost and deliver the body, then head to the guild. The clerk looks at you and hands over _(50*character_level)_ g each then tells you to lay low for a while since the cellar is particularly upset. It would be wise to wait until he receives a new playmate rather than to prance around the streets carelessly. You have failed the quest and do not advance a level.
_If you deliver the Elven letter:_ You reach a security outpost and deliver the letter, then head to the guild. The clerk tells you to leave and make sure to have your affairs in order in case of an inspection which should happen regularly. Also to keep a close eye in case a soldier wishes to plant a bogus charge, then he charges you _(15*character_level)_ g each for the advice. You have failed the quest and do not advance a level.

> Claims of people in the outskirts going missing were ignored until the first born of the captain of the guards who was traveling  the outskirts of the city was rumored to be taken away - he's not been seen since. Your job is to determine what's happening, why it's happening, and then put a stop to it!

The clerk leaves the documentation behind as she leaves. You take your documents and head to the entrance.

_You must first descend to your target level._

_Upon reaching the level, you must explore the rooms and trigger quest events. This way, you can begin the process of tracking down the location._

_Quest Encounter:_ You fight a Baby Dragon.

_Baby Dragon:_ _(dungeon_level+2)d6_ HP, Fire breath deals _(dungeon_level+2)d6_ on a single party member.

**Armored:** Baby dragons have tough skin that grants them _(dungeon_level+1)_ AC.

_Quest Event1 :_ Burn marks on the ground tell you that you're in the right vicinity. Though you feel for the victims, this time you're not as eager to find your target.

_Quest Event2 :_ You find treasure , but it's molten on the floor. Quite a travesty!

_Quest Event3 :_ You hear heavy breathing, luckily you notice a small hatch, and decide to crawl in. The breathing sound grows louder, this must not be a "typical" dragon, not that such niceties would make you at ease. You eventually reach a hatch, and lift it slowly, just enough to peer through the crack. The victims seem to be tied there. One of them is missing, well its arms are still there but the rest is gone. The others seem to be in one piece. You take your chances and gently open it to escape, you make a silence sign to the prisoners though they're mostly unconscious. To free them, you must unlock these chains, roll an unlock check 3 times. For each failed lock check, roll a body save; roll a hazard die upon failure.

_After freeing the prisoners:_ You manage to get into the hatch with them when you hear a deafening roar, it takes some time for the ringing to calm down enough to hear the thrashing that follows. At least you're in a good hiding place, but the beast knows where that is.

Roll _(dungeon_level+1)_ consecutive dex saves to sprint as quickly as you can out of here, each time you fail, fire damage will singe you causing _(dungeon_level+1)d6_ HP damage.

_After escaping the crawl space:_ You make a sigh of relief then hear a massive charge against the wall. It held, but for how long? You must keep running out of here! Make your way out of this level. Roll a hazard dice every square, each time you land on _2_, roll a body save or suffer _(dungeon_level +1)_ damage from the heat of the dragon increasing the temperature of the entire level. Once you reach the stairs, escape returns to normal.

_Upon reaching the surface:_ You take the victims straight to the pub and all of you have a cold beverage, it still feels like you are back in that furnace of a level. Then you go to the guild, and a security officer goes along with a clerk to help and collect their testimony. Meanwhile you receive a _(150*character_level)_ g reward each, the Guild master is about to protest about taking the victims to the pub then looks at the sooth in your clothes, hair and eyes and decides to hand you and additional _100_ g instead. This time, you simply go to bed and call it a day. Congratulations, you managed to level up.
