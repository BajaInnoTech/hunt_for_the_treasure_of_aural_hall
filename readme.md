# The Hunt for The Treasure of Aural Hall by BIT-Mysteries

Will you survive your next venture into the depths of the underground structure?

## What is this?

This is a **[DepthRangers](https://github.com/BajaInnoTech/depthrangers) RPG System Campaign**. Players must complete a series of quests that take place insinde a dungeon. The layout is designed procedurally by means of a series of random dice rolls.

It requires minimal setup, but provides variety within dungeon layouts and quest objectives & dynamics; blending some aspects of board gaming with RPG style character customization, progression and inventory.

## Where do you start

Players should start by reading the Beginner Adventurer's Guide, which is a step by guide that covers everything a player needs to get started.

## Do I need to read all of that to get started?

No, we designed the game with the following guiding principles:

* You get started playing immediately
* You learn things as you need them
* Explanations are short and clear

The game may look large, but most of the content is there to provide varied playing experiences - not complicated or long sessions.

However:

* As you level up, the games will become longer because your characters must travel longer distances. That said...
* The game is designed so you can pause at any time and resume play later.

Hence you should be able to have a meaningful experience in approximately 30 minutes.

## What is the game play actually like?

Players obtain quests, equip themselves then begin their adventures in the dungeon. Most of the time these quests are assigned to you at the guild by one of its many clerks or the guildmaster. Each time you successfully complete a quest, you level up (though unfortunately, there are some exceptions). And each time you level up, you must descend one level deeper to complete the next quest.

For each quest you will be given an objective within the dungeon. Upon completion of this objective, you must fight your way back to the surface before you can collect your rewards and hopefully level up.

The goal of the game is to acquire the legendary treasure at the deepest part of the dungeon then make it back to safety - preferably alive. Once players have already delved to level 4 and successfully completed a quest, they can aim for discovering the Final challenge, *"The Hunt for The Treasure of Aural Hall"* rumored to be at level 5.

For this initial release, we provide a limited series of quests. The aim being to provide one level of gameplay. Stay tuned as additional content will be provided regularly as we playtest them and prepare it for rollout. We have a lot already written, but it must be up to the same or higher standards before we release it.

## About the Depth Rangers System

The "**DepthRangers**" RPG system is focused on fast combat-heavy gameplay. It's a lean system that is flexible enough to provide varied playing experiences, and light RPG flavoring. We showcase some of its potential in this work.

### Origin of the System

In the process of development we fused our influences and ended up creating our own system which we called the "**DepthRangers**" RPG System. This engine was inspired by *Crawlers! Micro RPG* and *Dungeons and Dragons*.

The end result being a somewhat rules light RPG system that is leaner than something like *Basic Fantasy* but more complex than something like *Four Against Darkness*.
